SRC-04 · Trial dates — six days, not three

What this corrects. An account of the trial that gives only the 2023 sittings — January 11–12 and April 5, 2023 — and omits the 2022 sittings.

What the record shows. The certified caption of the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Order lists six trial dates under the word “Trial”:

  1. “May 12, 2022” [Dkt 396 p.1]
  2. “October 26, 2022” [Dkt 396 p.1]
  3. “October 27, 2022” [Dkt 396 p.1]
  4. “January 11, 2023” [Dkt 396 p.1]
  5. “January 12, 2023” [Dkt 396 p.1]
  6. “April 5, 2023” [Dkt 396 p.1]

Those are not merely settings. The Association’s interim trial briefs number the days from the first to the sixth:

  • “the general testimony presented during the first day of trial which took place on Thursday, May 12, 2022” [Dkt 326 p.2]
  • “the general testimony presented during the second day and third day of trial which took place on Wednesday, October 26, 2022 and Thursday, October 27, 2022” [Dkt 361 p.2]
  • “continued testimony of Mr. Whitmore to resume on Day 4 of the trial on January 11, 2023” [Dkt 361 p.12]
  • “fourth day and fifth day of trial which took place on Wednesday, January 11, 2023 and Thursday, January 12, 2023” [Dkt 383 p.2]
  • “The Court suspended further testimony at the end of Day 5, with the continued testimony of Mr. Pikor to resume on Day 6 of the trial on April 5, 2023.” [Dkt 383 p.10]

Read in sequence: Day 1 May 12, 2022; Day 2 October 26, 2022; Day 3 October 27, 2022; Day 4 January 11, 2023; Day 5 January 12, 2023; Day 6 April 5, 2023. The minutes corroborate that court sat on each date: “TRIAL COMMENCED” [Dkt 315 p.1]; “TRIAL RESUMED” [Dkt 349 p.1] and [Dkt 350 p.1]; “TRIAL RESUMED” [Dkt 363 p.1] and [Dkt 369 p.1]. The August 10–11, 2022 setting was continued, not held: “The parties have agreed to continue the trial in this matter from August 10-11, 2022 at 8:30 a.m. to October 26-27, 2022 at 8:30 a.m.” [Dkt 334 p.1]

Corrected wording. The trial was held on six days across 2022–2023: May 12, 2022; October 26 and 27, 2022; January 11 and 12, 2023; and April 5, 2023 [Dkt 396 p.1].

Scope. The NotebookLM lead attributes this list to the caption of Dkt 500. Dkt 500 is outside this site’s 1,121-document set, which ends at Dkt 495, so its caption was not read here. The list is proved from the certified Dkt 396 caption and the day-numbered trial briefs; the attribution to Dkt 500 remains unverified. The minute entries quoted above are saved JEFS docket text, not certified PDFs; they corroborate, and the certified caption governs.
