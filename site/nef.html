The two dates that do not — and why that is the interesting part

The mailbox has NEF mails dated 28-OCT-2026 and 29-OCT-2026 that match no NEF row in the Record on Appeal register — because the register ends at Dkt 495. Those two dates are exactly the dates of the post-495 tail entries published on /register (Dkts 496–503, filed 28-OCT and 29-OCT-2026).

So the mails corroborate the existence of Dockets 496–503 from outside the Record on Appeal: the tail is not an artefact of our own extraction — the court's mail system recorded filings on those days too.

What this mailbox does not contain — stated plainly

No appellate NEFs. The mailbox holds notices for the trial case (1RC191003776); it contains no notices for CAAP-26-0000625. The appeal-side cross-reference is therefore incomplete: nothing on /appeal is corroborated by this mailbox, and this page does not claim otherwise.

A missing mail is not evidence that a filing did not happen — only that this mailbox did not receive the notice. The docket remains the authority.
