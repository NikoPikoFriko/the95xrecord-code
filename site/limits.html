“Mr. Bigler admitted that he cannot guarantee that the conditions he observed on October 29, 2021 were the same as on May 9, 2018.” [Dkt 394 p.16]

The extracted page 13 of Dkt 392 carries printed folio 11.

3. The one inference on this page — labelled, with both sides

INFERENCE (not a finding, not a quotation). FOF ¶32 describes the elevator's condition on 9 May 2018 as “operating as it should without any defects”. The credited expert's described inspection happened on 29 October 2021 — three and a half years after the incident — and he admitted he cannot guarantee the 2021 conditions matched 2018. The court's finding does not state which evidence carried it beyond crediting Bigler. A reader may therefore ask whether the 2018 finding rests on a 2021 inspection; this record does not say so, and this page does not either. It presents the two anchors: [Dkt 396 p.9] for the finding and [Dkt 392 p.13] for the described inspection.
