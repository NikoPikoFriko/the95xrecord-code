The reading room publishes it as appeal Dkt 1 (id 10034ba1b7), and that text answers the open question: yes, the notice states the orders appealed from — three, in the caption (p.1) and again in the body (p.4). Grade stays OBS-LEAD: a published reading-room extraction, not a certified copy held locally. Its four quotations are NO-TEXT to the grounding checker, which carries no Dkt 496 text; that is a corpus limit, not a defect in the quotations.
  2. Timeline. Notice of appeal: 4 September 2026. Judgment on the flyleaf: 23 June 2026. Order denying reconsideration: 28 October 2026. The notice precedes the order, and the Record on Appeal names only the three earlier orders. How the appeal period was tolled is not stated in this layer; Dkt 500's holding bears on the question, but this page does not resolve it.
  3. The 5 October minute. Dkt 498 p.194 records “COURT FINDS MOTION TO REQUEST FOR EXTENSION OF TIME IS UNTIMELY.” That is a minute entry about Dkt 442; it is not an order dismissing the appeal, and it does not by itself establish the appeal's status.

Dkt 502's amended record nonetheless lists Revere & Associates as Pikor's attorneys (p.3) and records that the page 2 flyleaf was updated to show them, “removed Pro” [Se].
