Redaction rule — decision L-02. Court-record email exhibits are published with standard PII redaction: personal cell and home phone numbers, private home addresses and personal email handles are redacted; full names, timestamps and body text are kept. The masking is done with tools/redact.py, before anything is displayed here.

In practice: the eight published quotations are body sentences and contain no phone number, address or email handle — the tool reports 0 phones, 0 emails, 0 addresses inside them, so no quotation is altered in any way. The signature blocks and letterheads that do carry personal data are masked and are not reproduced. A filed signature line appears on this page only in this form: Cell: [PHONE REDACTED] · Fax: [PHONE REDACTED] · Email: [EMAIL REDACTED]. Over-redaction is the safe direction: the tool also masks business numbers and addresses on the exhibits (some invoice numbers read as phone numbers), and that is left alone rather than tuned.
